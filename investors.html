Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What exactly am I buying?

A physical asset — a container office, storage container, security cabin, labour accommodation unit or portable toilet — manufactured by RK Enterprise. You own that unit. You are not buying a share, a bond or a unit in any scheme.

Is the rental income guaranteed?

No. Every income figure here is an estimate, not an assured return. Actual income depends on the asset being successfully deployed, the rental agreement signed with the client, deployment location, contract duration and market demand. Please read the written terms before investing.

What is the minimum investment?

₹80,000 + GST for one 4×4 security cabin, with estimated rental income of ₹1,850 per month. There's no upper limit — diversified portfolios typically start around ₹15 lakhs.

Who rents my asset?

Verified industrial clients across construction, warehousing, infrastructure, factories, mining and government projects. RK Enterprise sources the client, signs the rental agreement and manages the relationship. You never have to find a tenant.

Who handles delivery, maintenance and repairs?

We do — manufacturing, delivery, installation, maintenance and client management. The asset is yours; the operational work is ours.

How long before my asset is built and deployed?

Most RK units are manufactured within 15–25 working days of order confirmation. Deployment timing also depends on client site readiness. We confirm expected timelines in writing for your specific order before you invest.

What happens between rental contracts?

Income accrues while the asset is deployed under an active agreement. Between contracts we work to re-deploy the unit. Ask us how idle periods, storage and re-deployment are handled for your specific asset, and get it written into the agreement.

Can I mix different assets in one portfolio?

Yes — most investors above ₹5 lakhs do exactly that, to spread across asset types and client industries. Use the calculator above and send us the combination you have in mind.

Is GST included in the prices shown?

No. Every investment figure on this page excludes GST. GST is charged as applicable and shown separately on your invoice, in line with our standard payment terms.

Can I sell the asset or exit later?

You own the unit, so resale is possible in principle. Exit terms, notice periods and any buy-back arrangement are not automatic — they depend on what's written into your agreement with us. Raise it before investing so it can be documented.

Can I see my asset being built?

Yes. You're welcome at our Bardhaman (WB) or Faridabad (Delhi NCR) factory to see units in production. Visits to a deployed asset can usually be arranged with notice, subject to the client's site access rules.
